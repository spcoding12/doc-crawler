## 2024-05-24 - Optimize BeautifulSoup queries in framework detection
**Learning:** Checking for elements one by one with `.select()` and `True` predicates is extremely slow in BeautifulSoup. Passing a list of class names to `soup.find(class_=[...])` acts as a fast OR operation during traversal and saves a massive amount of parsing overhead, especially on large pages. Also `script.text` is much faster and safer than `script.string` since it doesn't return `None` when tags are complex. Extracting meta tags specifically by attributes is much faster than generic selection followed by lambda evaluation.
**Action:** Replace sequential `.select()` or generic `soup.find_all('script')` loops in `src/detector.py` with optimized `soup.find(class_=...)` and targeted attribute searches. Use `.text` instead of `.string` for scripts.
